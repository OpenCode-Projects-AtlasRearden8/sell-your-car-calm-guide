1 Pricing — one sitting, ~30 min

  • Value checked on KBB, Edmunds, and live local listings (sold prices, not just asking)
  • Honest condition rating chosen — very good / good / fair
  • Ask price, target price, and walk-away price written down
  • Ask price is near the middle of current local listings
  • Car washed, vacuumed, personal items removed
  • Title, registration, records, spare keys gathered

Three prices, written down: Ask = market +5–10%. Target = market. Walk-away = market −10%. The walk-away is not renegotiated with myself on sale day.

2 Title & Bill of Sale — one sitting, ~1 hr

  • Physical title found (never kept in the glovebox)
  • Title in my name — or duplicate/transfer started with the DMV
  • No liens — or lender payoff called and release process started
  • Branding checked (salvage / flood / rebuilt); disclosure listed if branded
  • State notarization requirement looked up
  • Bill of sale drafted — 2 copies
  • Registration, owner's manual, maintenance records, both keys in one folder
  • Known-issues list written down (same story to every buyer)

Blue or black ink on the title, no cross-outs, no white-out. If my state requires notarization, I sign in front of the notary only.

3 Safe Test Drives — per buyer

  • Public daytime meeting spot chosen (police-station lot if available)
  • Buyer's name and phone number collected
  • Buyer's driver's license copied before the keys move
  • Test-driver coverage confirmed with my insurer
  • Title, wallet, and valuables NOT in the car
  • I drive the first few minutes; friend rides along if possible
  • Fixed route told in advance; I ride along the whole time
  • Buyer never drives the car alone — ever
  • Car re-locked, keys in pocket, flaws pre-disclosed
  • No deposit taken outside the Stage 4 rules

Cancel anyone who won't give a number, wants me to drive the car to them, or offers to pay via links, gift cards, or mystery "escrow" services.

4 Payment — sale day

  • Payment method agreed in advance and vetted
  • Cash counted in front of me, or teller-verified at a bank
  • Cashier's check issued at the bank in front of me — or verified by calling the issuing bank (number I looked up myself)
  • Funds fully cleared/settled before the title changes hands
  • No overpayment accepted — no "refund the difference," ever
  • Title back + bill of sale signed by both, in ink, copies photographed
  • My plates removed; keys handed over last

"Available" is not "cleared." A screenshot of "I sent it!" is not payment. Money in my account is payment — nothing else is.

5 DMV & Insurer — within 24 hours of sale

  • State notice-of-sale / release-of-liability form filed
  • Confirmation screenshot or certified-mail receipt saved
  • Insurer: old car removed (new car added first, if buying)
  • Pro-rated insurance refund confirmed
  • Plates returned or transferred per state rules
  • Toll transponder unlinked; subscriptions/auto-payments cancelled
  • Complete sale folder kept for records

Until the notice is filed with my state DMV, I am still legally on the hook for the car — tickets, tolls, even accidents. This step is not optional.
