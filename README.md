# Sell Your Car, Calm Guide

A step-by-step life-admin guide to selling a car privately — written the way a friend would explain it: plain English, one step at a time, never condescending.

Published by **Asdale Dynamics, LLC**.

## What this covers

1. **Pricing your car fairly** — no guessing, no underpricing
2. **Gathering the title and bill of sale** — the paperwork most people panic about
3. **Safe test drives** — screening buyers and protecting yourself
4. **Payment safety** — getting paid without getting scammed
5. **Notifying the DMV and insurer** — the step people forget, until it costs them

## Who this is for

Anyone selling their own car privately — first-timers especially. Low-energy and ADHD-friendly: every chapter ends with a short checklist you can do in one sitting.

## How to use it

**Just read the docs.** Open `docs/00-start-here.md` and follow along. Everything works offline — no accounts, no cookies, no data leaves your machine.

**Or use the website.** Open `site/index.html` in any browser. `site/checklists.html` is styled for printing — hit Ctrl+P and you have a paper checklist for the glovebox. There is no build step and no server required; it is plain HTML/CSS.

**Run the checks** (optional, for contributors):

```sh
python3 tests/run_tests.py
```

## Design principles

- **Local-first**: no tracking, no cookies, no JavaScript required, no network calls
- **Printable**: checklists are designed to print cleanly on one or two pages
- **Low-energy friendly**: chapters are short, checklists are one-sitting-sized
- **Education only**: general information, not legal or financial advice. Rules vary by state — always confirm with your local DMV and an attorney for anything unusual.
