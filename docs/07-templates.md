# 07 · Templates

Copy these, print them, keep them in the sale folder. They are generic templates — your state may have official versions (prefer those if they exist; search "[your state] DMV bill of sale").

## Template 1 — Vehicle Bill of Sale

Fill once, in ink, on sale day. Make **two copies**: one for you, one for the buyer.

```
                    VEHICLE BILL OF SALE

Date of Sale:      ______________________

Buyer:             First / Last:  ______________________
                   Address:       ______________________
                   Phone:         ______________________
                   State ID/License #: ____________________

Seller:            First / Last:  ______________________
                   Address:       ______________________
                   Phone:         ______________________

Vehicle:
  Make:            ______________________
  Model:           ______________________
  Year:            ______________________
  VIN:             ______________________
  Color:           ______________________
  Odometer reading (actual miles, no tenths):
                   ______________________
  Title number:    __________   State: ______

Sale price:        $ ________________
Payment method:    ________________________________________
                   (e.g., cashier's check #______ from ______ Bank,
                    verified and deposited, or cash counted in full)

Buyer statement:   I acknowledge that I have inspected this vehicle,
                   that it is sold AS-IS with no warranties, express
                   or implied, and that ownership transfers to me as
                   of the date above.

Seller statement:  I certify that I am the lawful owner of this
                   vehicle, that the odometer reading above reflects
                   the actual mileage, that the vehicle is sold
                   AS-IS, and that I have disclosed any liens or
                   title branding on this vehicle.

Buyer signature:   ______________________   Date: ______

Seller signature:  ______________________   Date: ______
```

*Note: a handwritten version is legal in every state as long as it names both parties, describes the vehicle (VIN), states the price, and is signed by both. Notarization is required in some states — check.*

## Template 2 — Notice of Sale (mail-in letter to your DMV)

Use this if your state handles notice-of-sale by mail rather than online. Find the right address on your DMV website.

```
                    NOTICE OF SALE OF MOTOR VEHICLE

Date: ______________

To: [State] Department of Motor Vehicles
    Vehicle Records / Registration Department
    [Address from your state DMV website]

RE: Notice that the vehicle described below was sold on
    the date indicated; request release of owner liability.

 Vehicle: ________________________ (Make/Model/Year)
 VIN:     ________________________
 Plate #: ________________________   State: ______
 Title #: ________________________

 Sold on:      ______________ (date of sale)
 Sale price:   $ ______________
 Buying party: ________________________ (name of buyer)
               ________________________ (buyer's address)

 Seller:       ________________________ (your name)
               ________________________ (your address)
               ________________________ (your phone/email)
 Driver's License / ID #: ____________________

 Signature:    ______________________   Date: ______
```

Send it **certified mail with return receipt** — the little green card is your proof you acted in time. Before mailing, check whether your state has a specific form number (e.g., California's REG 138 "Notice of Transfer and Release of Liability") — official forms process faster.

## Template 3 — Pre-Listing Inventory (glovebox folder)

Not legally required. Massively useful when a buyer says "do you have the second key?"

```
IN THE SALE FOLDER
[ ] Title (front + back copy of current state)
[ ] Registration (current)
[ ] Insurance card (for reference only; not given to buyer)
[ ] Maintenance records
[ ] Owner's manual
[ ] Bill of sale x2 (blank, to fill on sale day)
[ ] Notice-of-sale form (pre-addressed if my state mails it)
[ ] spare keys: ___ of ___ present, missing: ______
[ ] Known issues list (bumper scratch, ___ , ___)
```

Listing disclosure tip: write the known-issues list *once*, early, before the listing. Then paste it into every listing and repeat it at every test drive. Same story every time = trustworthy, and no one "wins" a gotcha negotiation.
