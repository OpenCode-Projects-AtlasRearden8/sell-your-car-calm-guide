# 04 · Payment Safety

**Goal of this chapter:** get paid in full, with money that cannot bounce, be reversed, traced-to-nobody, or faked. This is where car-sale scams live — read this even if you "know scams."

## The good ways to get paid

| Method | Verdict | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Cash, verified in person** | ✅ Best for smaller amounts | Count it, check bills, prefer branch deposit |
| **Cashier's check from a local bank, deposited in person** | ✅ Best for larger amounts | See below in detail |
| **Bank wire (buyer's bank transfers to yours)** | ✅ Good for large amounts | Confirm it has *settled* in your account |
| **PayPal/Stripe/Zelle from a bank you verify** | ⚠️ Only forshaled small balances | Zelle/Venmo/PayPal can be reversed after fraud; fine as partial deposit, risky for full payment |
| **Personal check** | ❌ Never | Clearing fake checks takes weeks |
| **Digital escrow site via emailed link** | ❌ Never | "Escrow" links are the top scam |
| **Gift cards, crypto, wire-to-strangers, overpayment** | ❌ Never | These are the scam itself |

## Detail: how to accept a cashier's check safely

1. **Watch it get made.** Best option: agree to meet *at the buyer's bank* while it is daylight and open, watch the teller print the check, then walk to *your* bank and deposit it the same hour. The check is literally never out of a real bank's hands.
2. **If it's already in hand:** call the issuing bank's number — **look it up yourself**, not the number printed on the check ("check-phone spoofing" is a thing) — and confirm check number and amount are live and real.
3. **Deposit, then wait for it to fully clear** (which can be several business days even when funds "available") before you hand over the title. An "available" balance is not a cleared check; a fake check can be pulled out of your account for the full amount days later.

## Detail: accepting cash

- Meet at your bank and have the teller verify and deposit. This is free, boring, and bulletproof.
- If in the field: reasonable checks are bill detectors ($10 hardware store), or simply comparing texture and watermark. Be extra careful above $2,000; counterfeit $100s circulate.
- Count in front of them before closing anything. Never let them "redo the count" or swap stacks.

## Detail: digital apps

- **Zelle** is effectively cash-like — instant, but reversals after legitimate-seeming transfers can happen with stolen accounts. Many banks won't protect you. Use only if the transfer has truly *arrived and stays* in your account.
- **Venmo/PayPal** goods-services is nice for a $500 deposit but a liability for the full price (reversal rules favor the buyer).
- Watch the classic move: a screenshot that says "I sent it!" is not payment. **Money in your account is payment. Nothing else is.**

## Detail: the overpayment scam (memorize this one)

Buyer sends $9,000 for a $7,000 car: "my mistake, please refund the $2,000 via gift card / Venmo / cash." You refund; then their original check bounces. You are now out $2,000 *plus* the bounce. Rule: **an overpayment is never refunded, it is canceled — the deal dies if it happens.**

## Sale-day sequence

1. Buyer arrives; agree on final price.
2. Payment happens **first** — verified, in your hands or your bank's count.
3. You and buyer fill out, in one sitting, in ink, both keeping copies:
   - the **back of title** (mileage, price, date, both signatures — notarized if your state requires)
   - the **bill of sale** (both copies)
4. Remove the plates from this car — keep your plates, then hand over *keys only*.
5. Photograph both signed documents. That's your proof-of-transfer backup.

Only then does the buyer drive away.

## Checklist — Payment Safety (read once, use on sale day)

- [ ] Payment method agreed in advance and vetted
- [ ] Cash: counted in front of buyer, or teller-verified at bank
- [ ] Cashier's check: issued at bank in front of me, or phone-verified with issuing bank
- [ ] **Fully cleared/settled in my account** before title changed hands
- [ ] No overpayment accepted (no "refund the difference" conversation)
- [ ] Title back + bill of sale signed by both, copies photographed
- [ ] Plates removed; title/keys handed over only after payment is safe
